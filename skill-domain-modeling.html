Skill card

domain-modeling

  • Learning outcome 1: Make a repository agent-ready: one-command checks, skills at project scope, AGENTS.md as pointers, a glossary and a standards file.

The pain point

The agent and the team use different words, so the output drifts. Matt Pocock says: "I would have to remind it, no, there's already a term for that".

What the skill does

It challenges terms that clash with the glossary or are vague. It writes each term into GLOSSARY.md as soon as you settle it. It also offers to record a hard decision.

When to skip it

  • You only want to look up a term. The skill is for when you are changing the project's terms, not just reading them in GLOSSARY.md.
  • You have a problem with the shape of a module, not with its words. The skill is for when the words are the problem.

Where the course uses it

  • Day 1, 10:45, lab 2, one-command build and test. You leave a GLOSSARY.md with 10 terms in your repo.
