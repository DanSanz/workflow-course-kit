Skill card

codebase-design

  • Learning outcome 2: Turn a written rule into a mechanical check, and put each check at the right layer.

The pain point

Modules are shallow and tangled, so they are hard to test. Matt Pocock says: "Usually a bad codebase is one that has a ton of shallow modules in it". A shallow module has a complex interface and not much implementation behind it.

What the skill does

It gives you and the agent one shared vocabulary for designing deep modules. A deep module has a lot of behaviour behind a small interface.

When to skip it

  • You do not yet know which code to redesign. The skill is for when you already know which module you are redesigning and need to think about its shape.
  • You have a problem with words, such as one term that means three things. The skill is for the shape of a module, not for the words of the domain.

Where the course uses it

  • Day 1, 13:15, lab 3, rules become checks. The lab covers deep modules.
